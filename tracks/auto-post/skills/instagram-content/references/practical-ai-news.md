# AI news readers can try

Use when the user or selected account brief wants AI news with an immediately usable example. Apply to both carousels and Reels. A research announcement, enterprise agreement or future product is still a valid news topic when explicitly requested; do not invent a consumer workflow to make it appear usable.

## Select the topic and example together

Check the new announcement and current official service/help pages. Prefer a feature the intended reader can access now. Record the announcement date in the audience's timezone separately from the edition date. Verify the service URL, sign-in requirement, age/region restrictions, plan and rollout status that affect the example. When access is paid or limited, make that condition visible beside the action. If the topic is open and no realistic example is available, choose another fresh story.

When the task asks for new or latest news, verify the original feature announcement before choosing it. A recently edited help page or a new tutorial about an existing feature does not establish a new launch. Use the requested freshness window, record what actually changed and why its original date qualifies, and select another topic when it does not. Keep timeless how-to posts as a separately selected format.

Lead with a familiar task: turn a report into a short presentation, revise a shop photo, compare a bill or plan a trip. Explain how the announced change helps that task. Pair the service with its actual entry point, input and next action; a product name alone does not tell the reader what to do.

For AI tools controlled through chat, write one complete copyable prompt that specifies the input, task, audience and desired output. Add one or two useful follow-up requests when they help the workflow. Keep the whole prompt readable on a carousel page or provide the full text in the caption/preview for a Reel. Export `prompt.txt` and any follow-up prompts alongside the assets and make them available from the existing preview page when there is one. Choose the count and format from the task; one example's five-minute talk and six slides are not defaults.

## Keep demonstrations accurate

Separate source-supported capabilities from authored prompt examples and actually tested results. A requested slide count, design style or time saving is not a measured outcome. If using an official demonstration, label it as an official demonstration and record frame timestamps and crops. Preserve product interface text; enlarge the relevant controls instead of fabricating a localized interface. A real test result needs the actual output, not a prompt rendered to look like a product screen.

Essential access conditions should be direct facts such as “무료 계정의 표준 한도” or “새 기능은 순차 제공”. Preserve source checks the reader needs, such as comparing generated numbers against the original. Avoid repeating imagined misunderstandings or appending a disclaimer to every page.

## Match the chosen format

- Carousel: give each page one useful job, such as the change, service entry, full prompt, review step, follow-up request or export. Vary layout according to the information and stop when the useful explanation is complete.
- Reel: keep the selected photographic and motion treatment while developing the example through meaningful stages. Make the service and action readable within the UI margins; put longer copyable text in the companion preview or caption.
- Account introduction: follow the selected carousel closing policy in [carousel.md](carousel.md). A brand closing is separate from the news explanation and does not replace the practical example.
