Tonlit is a paid photo editor focused on skin tone. There is no account, login or in-app purchase.

How to review:
1. Accept the terms on first launch.
2. On Home, drag the before/after slider, or tap "Try it on this photo" to open the built-in sample photo in the editor. "Choose a photo" opens the system photo picker and the camera button takes a new photo.
3. In the editor, the Reading tab shows the measured shade, with circles where the skin was measured. The Tone tab has recipes and a shade picker. The Skin, Light and Details tabs hold individual tools. Save writes the edited photo to the photo library (add-only permission).
4. Home > Colour draping compares four seasons of colours next to the face. Home > Match many photos applies one shade to up to 20 photos.

All processing runs on the device with Apple's Vision framework and two Google MediaPipe models (Apache License 2.0, credited in Settings > Open-source licences). No photos or personal data leave the device. The app describes shade numbers as an editing guide, not a diagnosis.
