SkillMerge: When Agreement Displaces Evidence
Research Forge · 19 October 2026 · Layout v20 · Research content v20
Compact working paper v20, 2026-10-19. Editorial reorganization of unchanged v19 evidence; no new experiment, 
independent data, native-method execution, model/API call, or professor endorsement.
Abstract
Does prioritizing agreement preserve useful evidence when two outputs are composed? We study 
this measurement question on an already exposed SciFact-derived bank, rather than claim a 
behavioral effect of executable skills. Stable common-item promotion is compared with the same 
round-robin order on 22,794 constructed ordered pairs across seven output capacities. Full-
containment gain/loss counts are 565/2700 at capacity one, 95/25 at two, and 0/2 at three; losses 
vanish from capacity four, while first-three scoring retains both failures. We derive a completion-
rank identity, an exact single-window characterization, and a conditional no-loss boundary for 
individually annotation-sufficient inputs of length at most three. In a fixed sufficient-input stratum, 
pooled and equal-component contrasts have opposite signs. Separate author verification preserves 
an initially overstrong failed check and its correction. All findings concern finite annotation 
contracts: independent skill histories, qualified pairs, and fresh behavioral evaluations remain 
absent, and the behavioral composition effect is null.
1 Introduction
Reusable skill files create a practical integration question: when two changes help separately, should 
both become available together? A lower joint score could indicate lost evidence, a changed ranking, 
an incomplete reference, extra context, or different resource permissions. These explanations require 
different repairs. We therefore audit the measurement and output-construction mechanisms before 
estimating interference between independently authored skills.
Our completed study manipulates sentence-ID lists with auditor-supplied correct labels. It neither 
runs skill files nor observes new agent predictions. This narrow setting allows exact comparisons under 
fixed inputs and references. Agreement means repeated IDs, not agreement between independently 
sampled reasoning paths or scientific claims. Figure 1 distinguishes the executed diagnostic lane from 
the unexecuted authoring, qualification, and fresh-evaluation lane.
The central question is whether promoting shared IDs changes completion of a reference alternative 
under a declared capacity. We retain failures, empty outputs, exposure, and nested denominators. 
The contribution is a reproducible mechanism audit and a conditional theorem, rather than a 
generally superior integration controller. Supplement S1 provides the reading map; S2–S7 make every 
assumption and counterexample explicit.
A ranking failure can still matter operationally when the application requires short evidence. 
Preserving the full union refutes only the stronger allegation that an annotated sentence disappeared 
altogether. Conversely, recovering a reference score after changing the grader does not establish a 
better deployed answer. Our controls identify these logical distinctions before a future study chooses 
an integration policy.

2 Related Work
SciFact and FEVER distinguish reference-supported correctness from other outcomes [1,3]. The 
pinned SciFact implementation slices the supplied rationale list to three IDs for rationalized abstract 
correctness [2]. ERASER distinguishes human-rationale agreement from predictive faithfulness [4]; 
our annotation containment is not its confidence-based sufficiency. CheckList motivates focused 
invariance and directional tests [5], here applied to deterministic contracts.
Multi-metric evaluation [6,7], construct validity [8,9], underspecification [11], dataset documentation 
[12], and benchmark selection [13] motivate explicit score-to-claim boundaries. Finite-data selection 
can itself overfit [21]. These sources motivate controls; none establishes a local behavioral composition 
effect.
DSPy compilation [10], conservative replay in SkillCommit [14], typed migration in SkillSmith [15], 
SkillCAT routing/assembly [16], and SkillEvoReg regression validation [17] supply substantive future 
comparisons. Instruction/resource controls [18] and the SkillsBench v1.1 release [19] address different 
budgets and environments. Self-consistency aggregates sampled final answers [20], whereas we reorder 
fixed evidence IDs. None of these native methods is executed here; their reported scores are not 
imported. All 21 inherited reference records retain their original inspection scopes, including abstract-
only reviews.
3 Problem and Method
For each evidence-bearing task, references are nonempty alternative sets of sentence IDs. A valid 
response is an ordered list of distinct in-range IDs. Full sufficiency accepts if any complete alternative 
occurs anywhere in the output. First-three applies the same test only to its first three IDs. Exact-
annotated requires equality with an enumerated alternative. These are separate operational endpoints, 
not interchangeable measures of semantic truth.
Round-robin scans A and B alternately, retaining each ID only on first appearance, to form a complete 
union order V. Stable promotion forms P by placing common IDs first while preserving V's relative 
order within both common and noncommon classes. Each output is then truncated to its first k IDs. 
Constructors read only A, B, and k; references enter post-hoc scoring and descriptive stratification. 
This fully stable rule differs from the earlier agreement constructor at small capacities, where common-
ID internal order can matter.
2

With one-based ranks and absent IDs assigned infinity, the first sufficient capacity is the smallest 
completion rank across reference alternatives. Consequently, each order passes exactly from its 
threshold onward. Two orders can differ only on one contiguous capacity interval, with one polarity. 
This identity depends on nested prefixes and full containment; it does not extend automatically to 
exact matching, first-three evaluation, or meaningful natural-language answers.
If each complete input contains a valid alternative and each has length at most three, stable promotion 
cannot lose full sufficiency at k at least four. Empty intersection leaves the baseline unchanged; two 
shared IDs bound union size by four; one shared ID leaves at most one exclusive omission, preserving 
the other input. No-loss does not mean universal success, and annotation-sufficient inputs are not 
independently Q-qualified skills.
Promotion preserves every common ID already selected by the baseline and can remove only 
noncommon IDs. A loss therefore requires all previously complete alternatives to intersect the removed 
IDs and no alternative to become complete in compensation. This certificate uses references after 
construction; it is an explanatory audit, not a deployable label-free selector or a claim that shared 
content is erroneous.

4 Experimental Setup
The unchanged exposed bank contains 309 evidence-bearing tasks, 204 recorded components, 2,494 
distinct inputs, and 22,794 ordered pairs. Diagonal and reversed pairs are retained as constructed 
views, not independent histories. Capacities zero through six produce 159,558 pair-capacity contracts, 
319,116 deterministic outputs, and 957,348 endpoint cells. These computations add no independent 
observations. Capacity counts output IDs; tokens, tool calls, and authoring costs are unmeasured.
The primary descriptive weighting averages pairs within tasks, tasks within components, then 
components equally. Pair-pooled and equal-task alternatives are reported separately. A fixed both-
input-sufficient stratum contains 12,067 pairs from 308 tasks and 203 components. It conditions on 
complete original inputs, not their k-truncated versions and not held-out behavioral qualification. We 
never reinterpret its inclusion rule as observed usefulness of executable skills.
Plans, code, eleven consumed inputs, results, and verifier snapshots are bound by hashes. A separately 
written prefix-set/bitmask checker covers 3,046 authored input pairs, 122,571 one/two-alternative 
reference families, and 857,997 reference-capacity cells. These certificates include overlapping alter­
natives and are not a native SciFact corpus replication. The same author wrote both implementations. 
Supplement S8–S12 preserves full denominators, commands, input locks, checks, and the failed 
verification attempt.
Exact enumeration removes sampling error within the declared finite constructions, not annotation 
error or uncertainty about external populations. Uniform-subset expectations are combinatorial con­
trols rather than sampled model trials. Repeated capacities, endpoint cells, and alternative reference 
families are computational denominators and never increase the number of independently qualified 
histories.
5 Results
Table 1 retains all 22,794 ordered pairs at every capacity. Full-sufficiency losses decrease from 2,700 
to 25 to two at capacities one, two, and three; capacities four through six have no gains or losses 
on this bank. First-three scoring nevertheless retains zero gains and two losses above capacity three. 
Thus extending the generated output and extending what the grader reads are different interventions. 
Figure 2 shows this separation.
A saved witness has A=(1,2,0), B=(4,5,0), and alternatives {1,2} or {4,5}. At capacity three, round-
robin outputs (1,4,2) and passes; promotion outputs (0,1,4) and fails. At four, (0,1,4,2) restores full 
containment but its first three IDs still fail. The two counted losses are ordered records, not two 
independent observed skill collapses. Across the bank, 3,387 pairs have different completion thresholds 
and 2,255 unions cannot contain any complete alternative.
Weighting changes the descriptive sign. In the fixed sufficient-input stratum at capacity one, gains 
565 minus losses 1,409 give pooled contrast -844/12067, approximately -0.06994. Equal-component 
contrast is +3363459013/266277211200, approximately +0.01263. Unequal response multiplicities 
explain why these summaries answer different questions; neither is selected retrospectively as the 
universal effect.

Inherited length controls reinforce this caution. Intersection-only has equal-component sufficient/
exact scores 0.290889/0.150539, below its pairwise length-matched uniform-subset control at 
0.356638/0.221823. Component-fold selection adds no score beyond its stable fixed choices. A four-
rule gold oracle bounds only its declared class; an excluded negative control can exceed it. Supplement 
S9–S10 preserves these comparison rights and their limitations.
Observed non-tie capacity intervals all have width one, but the theorem permits wider intervals 
under other references. The finite certificate finds minimal sufficient-input loss witnesses with union 
sizes three, four, and five at capacities one, two, and three; it finds none at zero or four through 
six. Exhaustive checking supports the declared bounded class, while the separate proof explains its 
assumptions. In particular, the bank's zero changes above three do not imply universal equality: the 
no-loss theorem allows gains and unchanged failures. These distinctions prevent a finite pattern from 
silently becoming a stronger general statement.
The original verifier incorrectly tested guaranteed success instead of no-loss for disjoint inputs. That 
frozen failed attempt remains recorded; a separately frozen corrected checker passes the registered 
implication without altering analysis outputs. This correction is author verification, not independent 
scientific replication.

6 Limitations
Annotations are neither complete semantic truth nor independently adjudicated deployment out­
comes. Correct labels are supplied, component separation does not erase prior exposure, and author-
constructed pair multiplicities do not estimate natural prevalence. The cap-four result depends on 
both sufficient inputs, length at most three, complete union construction, stable class orders, and full 
containment. Same-length changed output sets cannot have a reference-blind universal guarantee: a 
removed singleton supplies a counterexample.
4

Independent A/B authoring histories, Q-qualified pairs, reserved-S outcomes, native-method runs, 
and measured token costs remain zero or unavailable; the behavioral composition effect is null. A 
future comparison must freeze candidate classes, qualification, rights, resets, and total costs before 
fresh evaluation, allow conservative rejection/fallback, and retain zero yield, failures, and missingness. 
Supplement S13–S14 states the prospective decisions and falsification criteria; this editorial revision 
executes none of them.
7 Conclusion
Shared evidence can displace completion under a finite capacity without establishing general skill 
interference. Exact completion-rank analysis identifies when that happens, why full and first-three 
scores diverge, and which conditional boundary survives verification. Transparent weighting and a 
preserved verifier correction constrain the claim. Estimating executable-skill composition requires the 
still missing independently qualified behavioral study with complete resource accounting.
