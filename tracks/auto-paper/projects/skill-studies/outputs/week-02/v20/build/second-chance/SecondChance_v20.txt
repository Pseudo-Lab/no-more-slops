SecondChance: Auditing Recovery under 
Unknown Continuations
Research Forge · 19 October 2026 · Layout v20 · Research content v20
Compact working paper v20, 2026-10-19. Reanalysis and presentation of frozen authored evidence; no new model calls, 
actual audits, independent histories or native-method trials.
Abstract
Correcting an evaluator does not establish recovery of an integrated agent state. SecondChance 
studies which recovery comparisons remain identified when audits and future state transitions 
are incomplete. We distinguish worst scenario-conditioned width, the interval union when the 
continuation scenario is unknown, and its utility-blind pre-outcome maximum. An exact four-case 
objective yields two causal audit rules; a zero-sum L1 tube adds a common width term under 
explicit fresh-state conditions. Replaying 168 saved paths from twelve authored templates yields 
no positive realized ambiguity premium in 1,620 dependent panel views. All sixteen new-rule 
contracts tie their controls in final safe width; four checkpoint areas improve by 1/192. A late-query 
control attains width 1/24 rather than 1/12 but has worse uncertainty area. Increasing tube radius 
includes more of thirteen reused views without establishing calibration. No new audits, model 
calls or natural histories are added. The contribution is a bounded identification and comparison 
contract, not demonstrated real-agent recovery superiority.
1 Introduction
An evaluator patch can reverse an old rejection while leaving the current system broken. A saved 
correct candidate may not have been integrated; a harmful lesson may remain active; a valid 
later change may be lost during repair. Rescoring an archive, invoking a candidate and auditing 
the integrated state therefore answer different questions. Figure 1 retains this distinction without 
representing the authored recovery counts as language-model performance.
We ask a further question: how should an auditor allocate scarce state observations when future 
commits are unknown? A rule can optimize uncertainty conditional on a forecast while overlooking 
uncertainty about which forecast applies. A stronger objective can also improve a retrospective 
diagnostic without improving final information or justifying an operational speedup. The central 
comparison must separate these possibilities.
Our contribution is deliberately narrow. We derive three distinct width objectives, implement utility-
blind rules for unknown-scenario auditing, and preserve negative results under the same saved 
histories. The new rules change four intermediate-area summaries but none of sixteen final widths. A 
simpler delayed rule can win the final-width comparison while losing the area comparison. Complete 
proofs, causal-access contracts and historical results are routed to Supplement S1-S14; the full prior 
manuscript is preserved after those additions.

2 Related Work
SkillOpt, Reflexion, Self-Refine and CRITIC change skills, reflective memory or responses under dif­
ferent feedback contracts [1,2,3,4]. Intrinsic self-correction and SCoRe additionally differ in external-
feedback and training rights [5,6]. Verifier selection, temporal memory and environment interaction 
motivate preserving the candidate, lesson and tool state [7,8,19]. None is a native comparator executed 
in this study.
Proxy optimization, selection bias and adaptive holdout reuse motivate separating policy decisions 
from retrospective outcome inspection [9,10,11]. Variability and aggregate-evaluation studies motivate 
explicit experimental units rather than counting repeated views as independent trials [12,13]. Perfor­
mance profiles and COCO motivate distinguishing budget-limited quality, target attainment and 
nonattainment [14,15,16]; our checkpoint area is a declared local summary, not an imported runtime 
theorem.
Partial identification separates compatible-value bounds from statistical coverage [20]. Adaptive sub­
modularity requires structural conditions that our robust width-reduction objective need not satisfy 
[21]. EvalPlus and SWE-bench are possible future evaluation environments [17,18], not acquired 
benchmarks or sources of authenticated correction histories here. We retain all twenty-one references 
and their previously reviewed scopes; no new external effect size or theoretical guarantee is imported.
3 Problem and Method
Let B be a fixed recovery-cost horizon, t a checkpoint and r=(B-t)/B the remaining fraction. Across 
twelve equally weighted template pairs, p records the completed-prefix signed state occupations of 
neutral minus selective recovery. The current arm-frequency difference is d. A complete state has a 
deterministic fixed-input utility in [0,1]; one cached quote reveals the three fixed-panel utilities of a 
visible identity. Shared identities use the same utility variable.
The diagnostic continuation class is c(lambda)=p+lambda*r*d for lambda in [0,1], with endpoints 
c0=p and c1=p+r*d. It describes persistence followed by cancellation, not a fitted probability model. 
For an audited set A, first consider the worst remaining width after the scenario is fixed. Its value is 
W_cond below because the unqueried absolute-coefficient sum is convex in lambda.
Second, hold the actually revealed utilities fixed while allowing the scenario itself to vary. At endpoint 
i, Li is the known contribution plus all negative unknown coefficients; Ui adds all positive unknown 
coefficients instead. Their interval union has width W_union. Known contributions can move with 
the scenario even when every original utility is known, so W_cond and W_union need not coincide.
Third, deliberately ignore all utility payloads and maximize W_union over possible audited utilities, 
including earlier quoted values. This defines F and protects against all quote outcomes in the declared 
continuation class. Expanding an upper endpoint minus a lower endpoint leaves four ordered endpoint 
pairs; each known utility is maximized according to its coefficient difference. Supplement S3-S4 
supplies the exact separable formula and proof. New one-query and visible-set rules optimize F; their 
controls optimize W_cond with identical rights and tie rules.
Restricted objectives also need not have diminishing returns. In a four-state construction, either of 
two individual quotes has zero immediate worst-width gain, but their combination reduces width by 
one quarter. A one-query rule can therefore wait while a visible-set rule identifies a useful pair. This is 
a counterexample to an assumption about this objective, not a refutation of adaptive-submodularity 
theory or a claim of globally optimal online allocation. Supplement S8 preserves the construction and 
stopping rules.
A zero-sum coefficient perturbation with L1 radius epsilon=2*eta*r expands each width and F by 
exactly epsilon only when freely valued, unaudited fresh coordinates attaining zero and one are 
allowed. Positive and negative perturbation masses are epsilon/2. With a fixed eta and the same 
remaining fraction for every candidate, the addition is constant and cannot change rankings. A 
physically restricted continuation set can invalidate attainment. All actual sign certificates therefore 
retain the unrestricted prefix-plus-unknown-suffix bound, rather than treating the diagnostic class as 
established truth.

4 Experimental Setup
We reuse 168 already exposed paths from twelve authored templates and nine complete state identi­
ties. Horizons are B=5 and B=8; distinct-state quote caps are one through four. Nine declared causal 
rules give 72 dependent configurations, including sixteen new-rule contracts paired with corresponding 
restricted controls. The rules share arrivals and permit at most one visible-state quote per integer 
checkpoint, with no checkpoint-zero query. An audit never pauses the fixed recovery stream.
Selectors receive only arrived identities, timestamps, completed-prefix coefficients, current arm 
frequencies and their own query history. They receive no future commits, final coefficients, utility 
payloads or program semantics. Replacing hidden payloads while preserving the public prefix leaves 
decisions unchanged. Visible-set optimization is a static calculation followed by replanning, not a 
globally optimal causal policy.
The comparison records final unrestricted safe width, normalized left-step checkpoint-width area, 
quote count and certificate timing. Area sums widths at checkpoints zero through B-1 and divides by 
B. Quotes inspect saved values; their units are neither measured audit latency nor tokens. New actual 
audit executions, model calls, natural histories and independent units are all zero. Exact verification 
and counterfactual subset checks are distinguished from additional observations in Supplement S12.
5 Results
The two-state analytic example with endpoint coefficients (1/4,-1/4) and (-1/4,1/4), and known 
utilities (1,0), has W_cond=0 but W_union=1/2. This establishes a mathematical separation, not 
observed prevalence. On every one of the 1,620 actual dependent panel views, the realized union 
premium is zero. Counterfactual pre-outcome objectives can nevertheless differ because they range 
over unobserved quote outcomes.
The retained recovery experiment explains why fresh endpoints remain necessary: rescoring succeeds 
on all twelve saved cases while restoring no integrated state; candidate-selection procedures have 
twelve development-positive candidates but only four initial-state successes. Those earlier authored 
counts are not new evidence. A strong neutral joint control also matches selective recovery and has 
lower cost and collateral loss at budget five. Moving these details to the supplement preserves their 
constraints on the policy story rather than removing an unfavorable comparator.
All sixteen paired new-rule contracts tie their restricted controls in final safe width. Four B8 
checkpoint areas decrease by exactly 1/192, with the same query checkpoints and quote counts; 
twelve areas tie. At B8/q3, the one-query objective changes the state chosen at checkpoint five: its 
safe width changes from 7/8 to 5/6, while later widths coincide. The area difference is therefore 
(5/6-7/8)/8=-1/192. No additional unaffected-state certificate is obtained.
Table 1 preserves the timing reversal. At B8/q3, late-prefix queries at six, seven and eight and finishes 
at width 1/24, whereas FIFO, restricted-set and both union rules finish at 1/12. Yet late-prefix has 
area 75/64 rather than 37/32, a larger uncertainty burden by 1/64. Its positive unaffected certificate 
appears only at checkpoint eight. These are different endpoints; neither the smaller final width nor 
the smaller area alone identifies the better operational policy.
At eta values 0, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and 1, the same thirteen dependent nonterminal views have 
inclusion counts 4, 8, 10, 13, 13 and 13. Figure 2 makes this sensitivity visible alongside the four 
area changes. Inclusion is retrospective compatibility, not confidence coverage. Choosing eta after 
inspecting these final coefficients would not establish prospective calibration, and common tube 
inflation does not explain any policy-ranking improvement.

6 Limitations
The deterministic fixture was authored by the same research process that exposed its outcomes. 
Twelve templates, 168 files, repeated checkpoints and three panels do not supply independent natural 
correction histories. The original neutral-control advantages remain in the preserved manuscript. We 
claim no superiority over native SkillOpt, Reflexion, learned correction or deployed audit systems.
Identification requires complete commits, complete state identity and stable fixed-input utilities. 
Hidden mutation, stochastic audit disagreement or missing positive-duration observations must 
suspend an unsupported point estimate. The unrestricted suffix remains an outer bound for online 
information; restricted-segment membership is not assumed. Fresh-coordinate tube attainment need 
not represent a physically feasible transition process. The finite frontier covers nine declared rules, 
not all causal policies.
A confirmatory study must freeze policies and any eta calibration on separate histories, reserve 
independent correction families, preserve failures and nonattainment, and measure worker calls, 
failures and elapsed audit cost. Improved retrospective inclusion, checksum agreement or a model 
critic cannot replace that evidence.
7 Conclusion
SecondChance separates correcting a saved verdict from identifying current-state recovery, and 
separates three audit-width questions that can otherwise be conflated. Exact objective changes yield 
limited intermediate-area effects and no final-width gain in sixteen saved contracts. Delayed auditing 
trades terminal precision against accumulated uncertainty. Proof conditions, negative results and 
unchanged natural-evidence gaps are essential parts of the contribution.
