SecondChance: Exact Audit-Schedule Frontiers
Research Forge · 19 October 2026 · Layout v21 · Research content v21
Compact working paper v21, 2026-10-19. New exact structural schedule analysis of frozen authored streams; no new 
natural histories, actual audits, independent samples, model calls or native-method trials.
Abstract
Correcting an evaluator does not establish recovery of an integrated agent state. SecondChance 
distinguishes scenario-conditioned uncertainty, unknown-scenario union uncertainty, and a utility-
blind pre-outcome objective. We now compute the complete terminal-safe-width versus checkpoint-
area frontier over arrival-feasible audit schedules on fixed recovery streams. An exact audited-set 
dynamic program and a separate exhaustive verifier agree on 33,658 schedule-contract combina­
tions across eight horizon/cap settings. Each frontier contains one objective point: 28 of 72 saved 
causal-policy configurations match it and 44 are dominated. At horizon eight with three quotes, 
hindsight structure attains width 1/24 and area 73/64, dominating both FIFO and delayed 
auditing. This oracle uses future arrivals and occupations, never utility payloads; it is not an 
online policy. Prior negative findings remain: all sixteen union-rule final comparisons tie, four areas 
improve by 1/192, and realized ambiguity premiums are zero in 1,620 dependent views. No natural 
recovery, statistical coverage, or real audit-speed improvement is established.
1 Introduction
An evaluator patch can reverse an old rejection while leaving the current system broken. A saved 
correct candidate may not have been integrated; a harmful lesson may remain active; a valid 
later change may be lost during repair. Rescoring an archive, invoking a candidate and auditing 
the integrated state therefore answer different questions. Figure 1 retains this distinction without 
representing the authored recovery counts as language-model performance.
We ask how to allocate scarce state audits when future commits are unknown. Comparing a few 
declared policies can show a final-width versus uncertainty-area tradeoff without establishing that the 
tradeoff is unavoidable. We therefore ask a second, retrospective question: on the same fixed recovery 
streams, can any arrival-feasible schedule jointly improve both endpoints? The answer bounds what 
the declared schedule contract permits; it does not supply an implementable online rule.
Our exact structural oracle uses all realized arrivals and occupations but no utility values. Across 
eight contracts its frontier is a singleton, so the earlier nine-rule timing reversal is not an intrinsic two-
objective conflict on these streams. We retain the three uncertainty objectives, negative comparisons, 
thirteen dependent membership views, and historical recovery controls. Supplement S1-S14 preserves 
prior derivations; S15-S19 supplies the complete schedule analysis and new reading resources.

2 Related Work
SkillOpt, Reflexion, Self-Refine and CRITIC change skills, reflective memory or responses under dif­
ferent feedback contracts [1,2,3,4]. Intrinsic self-correction and SCoRe additionally differ in external-
feedback and training rights [5,6]. Verifier selection, temporal memory and environment interaction 
motivate preserving the candidate, lesson and tool state [7,8,19]. None is a native comparator executed 
in this study.
Proxy optimization, selection bias and adaptive holdout reuse motivate separating policy decisions 
from retrospective outcome inspection [9,10,11]. Variability and aggregate-evaluation studies motivate 
explicit experimental units rather than counting repeated views as independent trials [12,13]. Perfor­
mance profiles and COCO motivate distinguishing budget-limited quality, target attainment and 
nonattainment [14,15,16]; our checkpoint area is a declared local summary, not an imported runtime 
theorem.
Partial identification separates compatible-value bounds from statistical coverage [20]. Adaptive sub­
modularity requires structural conditions that our robust width-reduction objective need not satisfy 
[21]. EvalPlus and SWE-bench are possible future evaluation environments [17,18], not acquired 
benchmarks or sources of authenticated correction histories here. We retain all twenty-one references 
and their previously reviewed scopes; no new external effect size or theoretical guarantee is imported.
3 Problem and Method
Let B be a fixed recovery-cost horizon, t a checkpoint and r=(B-t)/B the remaining fraction. Across 
twelve equally weighted template pairs, p records the completed-prefix signed state occupations of 
neutral minus selective recovery. The current arm-frequency difference is d. A complete state has a 
deterministic fixed-input utility in [0,1]; one cached quote reveals the three fixed-panel utilities of a 
visible identity. Shared identities use the same utility variable.
The diagnostic continuation class c(lambda)=p+lambda*r*d, lambda in [0,1], has endpoints c0=p 
and c1=p+r*d. It is persistence followed by cancellation, not a probability model. Worst scenario-
conditioned width W_cond sums unknown absolute coefficients after fixing the scenario. Actual union 
width W_union also allows the scenario-dependent known contribution to move. Maximizing that 
union over every possible audited utility gives a separate utility-blind objective F, with an exact four-
endpoint-pair formula in Supplement S3-S4.
A four-state example violates diminishing returns for the restricted worst-width objective: either 
singleton quote has zero gain, but their pair reduces width by one quarter. Visible-set rules can 
exploit such complementarity without being globally optimal online policies. Under a zero-sum L1 
tube of radius epsilon=2*eta*r, freely valued fresh coordinates attaining zero and one add exactly 
epsilon to each width and F. Fixed common inflation therefore cannot change candidate rankings. 
All certificates still use the unrestricted safe width W_t below.
For the complete schedule problem, A_t contains identities quoted by checkpoint t. A legal schedule 
may skip or reveal one not-yet-quoted identity that has actually arrived by each integer t=1..B, 
using at most q quotes overall. There is no checkpoint-zero quote. Disclosure is instantaneous at a 
checkpoint and never changes or pauses the recovery stream. Thus terminal queries change W_B but 
contribute no left-step area.
Dynamic programming retains the smallest cumulative area for each checkpoint and audited set. 
Two paths reaching the same state have identical future options and terminal widths; the larger 
accumulated area cannot improve a continuation. At B, we discard points dominated in both terminal 
width and area, retaining equal-objective multiplicities and a deterministic schedule witness. This is 
exact for the fixed structural path and stated clock. Seeing future arrival and occupation structure 
remains privileged even when the computation is entirely utility-blind.

4 Experimental Setup
We reuse 168 exposed paths from twelve authored templates and nine complete state identities. The 
contrast uses twelve equally weighted neutral/selective pairs per horizon. B is five or eight, and quote 
caps q range from one to four. All schedules share actual arrivals, the fixed recovery path, globally 
cached identity quotes and the same instantaneous-disclosure convention. The two horizon streams 
and nested caps do not create independent trials.
Selectors receive only arrived identities, timestamps, completed-prefix coefficients, current arm 
frequencies and their own query history. They receive no future commits, final coefficients, utility 
payloads or program semantics. Replacing hidden payloads while preserving the public prefix leaves 
decisions unchanged. Visible-set optimization is a static calculation followed by replanning, not a 
globally optimal causal policy. The oracle alone sees the full future structural path; its rights differ 
from every causal rule.
Before computation we copied plan, analysis, verifier and 172 input files into a new run and sealed 
hashes. The dynamic program used 4,458 transitions. A separate implementation reconstructed 
coefficients from integer occupation intervals and exhausted every legal schedule in all eight contracts. 
It rejected fourteen invalid schedules and exactly reproduced 72 old terminal/area rows. Counts 
total 33,658 schedule-contract combinations, including nested-cap repeats; at maximum cap there are 
25,904 distinct horizon/schedule pairs. No actual audit, natural history, independent unit, model call 
or physical latency measurement was added.
5 Results
The two-state analytic example with endpoint coefficients (1/4,-1/4) and (-1/4,1/4), and known 
utilities (1,0), has W_cond=0 but W_union=1/2. This establishes a mathematical separation, not 
observed prevalence. On every one of the 1,620 actual dependent panel views, the realized union 
premium is zero. Counterfactual pre-outcome objectives can nevertheless differ because they range 
over unobserved quote outcomes.
Every complete schedule frontier contains one objective point. At B5, the terminal-width/area pairs 
for q=1..4 are (1/3,31/25), (1/5,6/5), (2/15,6/5), and (1/15,6/5). At B8 they are (1/8,75/64), 
(1/12,37/32), (1/24,73/64), and (0,217/192). These are exact saved-stream outcomes, not a general 
theorem that all audit frontiers collapse. Twenty-eight of 72 causal-policy rows match their frontier; 
44 are dominated under these two axes and the same cap.
At B8/q3, the structural witness quotes at checkpoints one, two and four. Its final width is 1/24 
and area 73/64, jointly beating FIFO and both union rules at (1/12,37/32). Relative to that witness, 
their terminal gap is 1/24 and area gap 1/64. Late-prefix matches width 1/24 but has area 75/64, a 
gap of 1/32. The earlier timing reversal among declared rules remains true, but does not establish a 
necessary conflict across all schedules. A witness satisfying arrival constraints need not be selectable 
without future structure.
At B8/q4, no declared rule reaches the structural point (0,217/192). Current-disagreement spends 
only two available quotes and remains worse in width and area; unused allowance is preserved. Frontier 
equality holds for some B5 rules and B8 FIFO at q1/q2. Ties, nonattainment and all 72 comparisons 
remain explicit in Supplement S18. This two-axis fixed-cap frontier differs from the earlier nine-rule 
frontier that also minimized realized quote count.
Prior negative findings remain unchanged. All sixteen union-rule contracts tie restricted controls in 
final safe width; four B8 checkpoint areas decrease by 1/192 and twelve tie. Late-prefix's unaffected 
positive certificate still occurs only at checkpoint eight in the B8/q3 case. At eta 0,1/8,1/4,1/2,3/4,1 
the same thirteen dependent nonterminal views have inclusion counts 4,8,10,13,13,13. Figure 2 
retains this sensitivity: compatibility is not statistical coverage, and uniform inflation cannot improve 
rankings.
The earlier recovery controls also remain adverse: rescoring accepts all twelve saved cases while 
restoring no integrated state, and twelve development-positive candidates yield only four initial-state 
successes. A neutral joint control matches selective recovery with lower budget-five cost and collateral 
loss. Those inherited authored observations limit the recovery story; they are not new v21 evidence.

6 Limitations
The deterministic fixture was authored by the same research process that exposed its outcomes. 
Twelve templates, 168 files, repeated checkpoints and three panels do not supply independent natural 
correction histories. The original neutral-control advantages remain in the preserved manuscript. We 
claim no superiority over native SkillOpt, Reflexion, learned correction or deployed audit systems.
Identification requires complete commits, complete state identity and stable fixed-input utilities. 
Hidden mutation, stochastic audit disagreement or missing positive-duration observations must 
suspend unsupported point claims. The unrestricted suffix remains a conservative outer bound; 
restricted-segment membership is not assumed. Fresh-coordinate tube attainment need not be physi­
cally feasible. The complete schedule frontier covers only the fixed recovery streams and two stated 
axes, with no query-duration feedback. It is not an optimum over online policies, altered recovery 
actions, real latency or other quote prices.
A confirmatory study must freeze policies and any eta calibration on separate histories, reserve 
independent correction families, preserve failures and nonattainment, and measure worker calls, 
failures and elapsed audit cost. Improved retrospective inclusion, checksum agreement or a model 
critic cannot replace that evidence.
7 Conclusion
SecondChance separates corrected verdicts from integrated-state recovery and causal audit choices 
from privileged structural bounds. Exact enumeration shows that the declared-rule timing reversal 
need not survive a complete schedule comparison on the same stream. It does not identify an online 
repair: future structure, complete deterministic state, real audit costs and new independent histories 
remain substantive admission gates.
