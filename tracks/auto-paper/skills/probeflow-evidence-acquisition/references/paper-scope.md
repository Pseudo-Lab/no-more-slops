Overall empirical acquisition outcome remains **no_gain**, with scientific status **needs_evidence**. Positive finite-law certificates and improved predictors do not establish useful acquisition superiority over strong matched static controls.

Both CAL policies satisfy mean coverage .50 and minimum-person coverage .25. The predefined crossvalidation rule selects static. These are reused development people, not a new independent final cohort.

The label-informed CAL-class oracle diagnoses potential versus transfer excess. It is privileged analysis, not an achieved deployable learner. Its corrected arithmetic bounds are separate from the primary method review, which retains an evidence gap. Document acceptance, numerical checks and model agreement do not replace independent scientific evaluation.
